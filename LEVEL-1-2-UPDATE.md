# Level 1 and 2 information workflow

This is the full public GitHub Pages edition, including the latest endorsement update. Upload the contents at the repository root, preserving folders. Do not upload this edition over the protected Netlify/Supabase repository.

Level 1: save partial student/academic details, or confirm complete intent information. Level 2: choose a company on record or enter a new one; track Not applied yet, Awaiting response, Accepted, or Not proceeding. Pending applications require only the company name. Accepted applications require complete contact details and a direct Google Drive acceptance-file link.

Levels 1 and 2 no longer request duplicate Google Forms information or downloadable information forms. Endorsement and subsequent document submissions remain available. Saved information is reused by document preparation.

Information is local to the current tab unless Remember my details is selected. It is not sent to the coordinator, synced between devices, or an official approval. Three stars on these two levels mean complete information confirmed by the student; pending company responses remain at two stars. Other levels retain clearly labeled demonstration progress. No deployment permission is implied. Google Forms responses and previously downloaded documents are not edited or deleted.

After changing company, review and regenerate affected documents. Existing submitted files remain unchanged. Clear all details when using a shared device.

Validation: 17 document-generation tests plus 3 workflow tests pass. Physical mobile and live deployment testing were not performed for this update.
